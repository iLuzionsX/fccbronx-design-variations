§ 01

Wall text

Vision

Welcome to Fordham Community Church. We are a protestant church in the South Bronx with a heart to see a Kingdom Family that displays the love of Jesus flourish in Fordham, Kingsbridge, the Bronx, and beyond.

Join us as we gather to sit under the Word, in worship and fellowship, and to see our neighborhood transformed by Jesus.

We want to be known as more than just a Fordham church or a Kingsbridge church — we want to be known as a Bronx church. We love our borough and firmly believe the Bronx isn't absent from God's peace and security. The best church for the Bronx is one that embraces the borough as a whole and works from within it to see the Gospel bring transformation.2

§ 02

Three commitments

Values

We value expository preaching of God's word, believer's baptism, biblical church leadership, meaningful membership, and a deep longing to see the Bronx filled with the glory of Jesus. We press into these values through three things we believe are crucial to thriving as a church in the Bronx.
