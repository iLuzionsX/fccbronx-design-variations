01

Vision

Building a Kingdom Family that displays the love of Jesus.

Welcome to Fordham Community Church. We are a protestant church in the South Bronx with a heart to see a Kingdom Family that displays the love of Jesus flourish in Fordham, Kingsbridge, the Bronx, and beyond.

Join us as we gather to sit under the Word, in worship and fellowship, and to see our neighborhood transformed by Jesus.

Come worship with us →
02

Values

We value expository preaching of God's word, believer's baptism, biblical church leadership, meaningful membership, and a deep longing to see the Bronx filled with the glory of Jesus. We press into these values through three commitments.

  1. No. 1

    Know

    Knowing God's word and the Gospel.

  2. No. 2

    Live

    Living like family, following Jesus together.

  3. No. 3

    Love

    Loving our neighbors in word and deed.
