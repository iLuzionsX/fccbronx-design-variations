01

Vision

Building a Kingdom Family that displays the love of Jesus.

Welcome to Fordham Community Church. We are a protestant church in the South Bronx with a heart to see a Kingdom Family that displays the love of Jesus flourish in Fordham, Kingsbridge, the Bronx, and beyond.

Join us as we gather to sit under the Word, in worship and fellowship, and to see our neighborhood transformed by Jesus. We want to be known as more than a Fordham church or a Kingsbridge church — we want to be known as a Bronx church.

02

Values

We value expository preaching, believer's baptism, biblical church leadership, meaningful membership, and a deep longing to see the Bronx filled with the glory of Jesus. Three commitments carry that forward.

  1. 01 / 03

    Know

    Knowing God's word and the Gospel.

  2. 02 / 03

    Live

    Living like family, following Jesus together.

  3. 03 / 03

    Love

    Loving our neighbors in word and deed.
